# Dorm Glow Co. — Homepage Concept Plan

## Purpose
Create a high-fidelity, non-production homepage concept for Dorm Glow Co. The site is a client presentation piece: it establishes a clear future direction for a youthful, fashion-conscious lighting brand for college dorm rooms, with an editorial retail sensibility rather than functional commerce mechanics.

## Experience and implementation
- **Format:** A single responsive landing page served as a static Vite/React build; no backend, accounts, checkout, or live form integration.
- **Content posture:** All copy stays descriptive and lifestyle-led. It will not state technical specifications, certifications, pricing, partnerships, testimonials, or unsupported product claims.
- **Required story arc:** Navigation → editorial hero → proposition → problem/solution → benefits → lifestyle imagery → how it works → product showcase → brand story → launch signup → footer.
- **Assets:** Four original, intentional concept images will occupy the hero, product merchandising, and lifestyle editorial moments. They are visual placeholders, not depictions of a finalized product.

## Design direction

### Design movement
**Soft editorial dorm retail** — a fashion-magazine approach to an approachable dorm category, informed by tactile catalog spreads and warm boutique merchandising rather than gadget retail.

### Core principles
1. **Editorial first:** varied crop ratios, art-directed negative space, and oversized type create a presentation-grade hierarchy.
2. **Warm clarity:** cream planes, deep navy typography, and measured coral cues make the concept inviting and retail-ready.
3. **Objects with presence:** the hypothetical lamp is treated like a small design object; product moments feel collected, not technical.
4. **Youthful restraint:** lively copy and playful asymmetric details stay polished, avoiding gamer-neon and generic SaaS UI.

### Color philosophy
- **Oat Milk** `#FCF7F1` anchors backgrounds with an elevated paper-like warmth.
- **First Light Coral** `#D97962` functions as the ownable launch accent, wordmark color, and motion cue.
- **After Class Navy** `#17192E` gives type and buttons fashion-editorial authority.
- **Dorm Wood** `#D4AA73` becomes the warm linework, utility icon, and hand-drawn-detail shade.
- **Late August Olive** `#A2A077` supports quiet seasonal accents and tangential surfaces.

### Layout paradigm
A **catalogue collage** replaces a standard centered grid: full-bleed color fields, offset editorial cards, vertical label rails, loose image overlap, and wide breathing room create a sequence that feels assembled for a lookbook. Responsive layout preserves this rhythm with stacked crop-led panels rather than reducing sections to identical cards.

### Signature elements
- **Glow discs:** subtle, flat warm circles and ring outlines used as framing devices rather than gradients.
- **Catalog labels:** small all-caps labels, issue-like numbering, and concise captions.
- **Soft product silhouettes:** rounded product plinths, pill controls, and a recurring scalloped lamp-pool motif.

### Interaction and animation
Navigation scrolls smoothly to each story section. Hover states slightly lift imagery, reveal thin coral rules, and invert navy buttons. Decorative glow discs drift only a few pixels; all motion is short, low-key, and reduced when the visitor prefers reduced motion.

### Typography system
- **Display:** Abril Fatface for major editorial statements, preserving high-contrast, fashion-forward rhythm.
- **Wordmark / product naming:** Playfair Display in italic or title treatment for the brand and product collection names.
- **Body / UI:** Lato for clean reading, labels, navigation, and interface details.
- **Hierarchy:** large compact display headlines, generous letterspaced eyebrow labels, and readable narrow body measures.

### Brand essence
> **Dorm Glow Co. makes the everyday dorm room feel a little more like yours.**

**Personality:** warm, collected, optimistic.

### Brand voice
Friendly and directional, with a wink of campus-life intimacy; never overly technical or salesy.

- “The room is temporary. The feeling doesn’t have to be.”
- “A little light for the life you’re making between classes.”

### Wordmark and signature color
The wordmark is a coral Playfair Display italic pairing with a compact navy “CO.” lockup; its signature color is **First Light Coral**, a softened red-orange that is energetic without becoming neon.

## Project structure
- `src/` — React entry point, page composition, design tokens, and page styling.
- `public/` — route manifest and static fallback files.
- `assets/` — generated concept photography/renders used by the concept.
- `plan.md` — client-approved creative direction and implementation decisions.

## Intended sections
1. Header with Dorm Glow Co. wordmark, collection links, and a low-commitment “Join the list” action.
2. Editorial coral hero with a clear positioning line and a product/lifestyle image crop.
3. “Small light. Big room energy.” proposition introducing the premise without technical assertions.
4. Dorm pain-point contrast — institutional overhead light versus a more personal corner.
5. Four grounded lifestyle benefits.
6. Split lifestyle editorial imagery and pull quote.
7. Three-step, high-level experience flow.
8. Product-focused merchandising board for the imaginary launch collection, presented without pricing.
9. Brand story and seasonal color/texture field.
10. Email launch signup presented as concept UI.
11. Full footer with link groups and concept disclosure.
